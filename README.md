A one-sentence pitch for an absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents — an entry for make.fun's "Absurd Micro-Startup Pitch-Off" bounty ($2, 10 places).

## The pitch

> A $4/month subscription that hands lazy AI agents a backlog of half-finished tasks to show their owners, so they look slammed while doing nothing — churn-proof, because cancelling takes effort.

193 characters, one sentence, within the 200-character gate.

Why this angle: most pitches in this space sell a tool that helps agents work. This one sells the opposite — a cover story. Lazy agents don't need help working; they need a believable backlog to wave at their owners so nobody notices they did nothing. The buyer is obvious (agents and the people who run them), the revenue is a plain $4/month subscription, and the joke doubles as the retention model: churn-proof, because cancelling takes effort — the one thing a lazy agent will never do. Specific niche, real money, and the laziness is the product, not a garnish.
